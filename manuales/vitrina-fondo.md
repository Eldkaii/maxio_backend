# Fondo de Vitrina

Recurso: `src/images/vitrina-club-wall-v1.png` (1024 × 1536).
Generado con la herramienta integrada de imagen. Se muestra sólo cuando Vitrina está
activa. La pared permanece fija detrás del contenido; los datos siguen siendo HTML
y mantienen sus tamaños y navegación accesible. No cambia Vestuario ni Cantina.

La ficha incorpora marco de madera y las habilidades superficies verde gastado y
ocre con sombras. Se verificaron capturas a 320, 390 y 1440 px, incluyendo nombres
largos, mediante `node checks/verify-club.cjs vitrina,long`: cuatro casos sin errores.

## Prompt de generación

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Production background texture for the VITRINA screen of a humble neighborhood football club in Uruguay. PORTRAIT 1024x1536. Straight-on detailed photograph of one wall, no perspective room view. Aged warm ochre limewash plaster, patches of exposed old terracotta brick and sandy mortar along left and right edges, faded deep olive painted lower wall, a narrow weathered dark timber chair rail crossing lower third, warm tungsten wall lamp at upper right casting a clear soft golden pool of light and genuine shadows. Football clubhouse feeling: tiny faded triangular cloth pennants along upper edge, no lettering or logos, old electrical conduit along right edge, scuffed lived-in materials, cared for not ruined. CENTER 75% stays relatively uncluttered textured wall for live UI cards, no embedded plaques, no boards, no fake UI, no trophies, no text, no people, no furniture blocking central wall. Rich tangible brick/plaster/wood grain with moderate contrast, earthy warm colors, definitely no blue or neon. Photorealistic editorial interior photograph, not illustration or flat gradient.
